About me

Engineering student, former ERP consultant and long-time boulderer.

I'm Jack Vermaak, an Infotronic Systems Engineering student at Hochschule Rhein-Waal in Germany. The degree combines electronics, computer engineering and software, and I expect to finish in late 2027.

I'm most interested in where hardware and software meet: what runs on a microcontroller, what runs on a computer, and how the two talk to each other. Recent projects include a USB audio controller, a click-to-screen latency tester, a software radio receiver and the electrical system for a hydrogen-powered excavator.

I grew up in South Africa and spent two and a half years in Cape Town as a Workday ERP consultant before moving to Germany to study. That work taught me to gather requirements from clients, document processes, test changes carefully and track down data problems.

Outside of study and work I've been bouldering for over 12 years, with several outdoor problems up to V11 (8A).
