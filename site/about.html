About me

Engineering student, former ERP consultant and long-time boulderer.

I am Jack Vermaak, an Infotronic Systems Engineering student at Hochschule Rhein-Waal in Germany. The degree combines electronics, computer engineering and software.

My main interest is where hardware and software meet: what runs on a microcontroller, what runs on a computer, and how the two communicate. Recent projects include a USB audio controller, a click-to-screen latency tester, a software radio receiver and the electrical system for a hydrogen-powered excavator. On the software side, I have built a 7-day bouldering conditions forecast and a site that shows Adam Ondra's ascents on his own grading scale.

I grew up in South Africa and spent two and a half years in Cape Town as a Workday ERP consultant before moving to Germany to study. That work taught me to gather requirements from clients, document processes, test changes carefully and resolve data problems.

Outside of study and work, I have been bouldering for over 12 years, with several outdoor problems up to V11 (8A).
