Hydrogen-powered excavator

Converting an old electric excavator to run on a battery charged by a hydrogen fuel cell.

A university project across two campuses to take a plug-in electric excavator that had been unused for about ten years and make it run without a mains connection. A hydrogen fuel cell charges a 48 V LiFePO4 battery, and the battery drives the motor that powers the hydraulics.

I was one of four students in the electrical engineering group during the design phase. We chose the battery, a separate battery management system and the voltage converters, and designed the control platform: a Raspberry Pi coordinating several Arduinos that talk to the excavator's parts over CAN bus.

Keeping it safe

The Raspberry Pi is not designed for safety-critical work, so the shutdown logic runs on the Arduinos. If the Pi crashes or loses contact, they can still switch the system off on their own. We also chose to monitor the hydraulics rather than control them from software.

Outcome

The team built the excavator during a five-day Makeathon in February 2026, and it ran successfully. I also helped design the project poster and presented the project at the university.
