Virtual reality card game

A card duel you play with your hands in VR.

A team project for the Innovative Technologies course. Four of us built a card duel game in virtual reality with Unity and Oculus XR. You stand at a table, physically pick up cards and play them against a computer opponent.

The project got a 1.3, the highest grade in the class.
